## Standing rules, verbatim from the owner

- "Verify latest data at source, do not infer from cadence."
- Disclosure control is non-negotiable: counts below 6 suppressed and shown as "<6, suppressed for disclosure control"; a suppressed count must leave no published field from which it can be recovered; rates hidden where the denominator is below 100; every decision logged; source suppression propagated as inherited. Never emit, infer or reconstruct a suppressed value. Individual-level data is out of scope, always.
- Rates are always recomputed from counts at the level displayed, never averaged from a lower level's rates. Never fill a district with its county's value. A force-level indicator says so rather than showing the parent force's value as if local.
- Refresh PRs: no auto-merge. A published bundle is never rewritten in place.
- The Corrections section records defects in published figures only.
- House style: UK English, sentence-case headings, no em dashes anywhere including code comments, metric units, verified facts only, no LLM clichés. Stan is never "Professor".
