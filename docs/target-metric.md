# PRISM-R target metric: tracking the 25% remand commitment

The Youth Justice White Paper Cutting Youth Crime. Changing Young Lives. (MoJ, 18 May 2026) commits to reducing custodial remand for children by 25% over this Parliament. The document uses two phrasings: "the number of children remanded to custody" (a flow) and "the custodial remand population" (a stock). Parliamentary discussion on 18 May 2026 treated it as the stock. No operational definition has been published. PRISM-R therefore tracks both, states which is which, and will adopt the MoJ definition if one is issued.

## Headline tracker: custodial remand population (stock)

Children under 18 held in youth detention accommodation on remand, whole secure estate, from the monthly Youth Custody Service report. Reported as a 12-month rolling average to remove seasonality. Baseline: the twelve months to March 2025. Markers shown for February 2026 (the figure cited in Parliament) and 18 May 2026 (White Paper). Refreshed monthly.

## Accountable measure: remands to youth detention accommodation (flow)

Episodes per year from Youth Justice Statistics, chapter 6. Baseline: 988 in the year ending March 2025. A 25% reduction is 741 or fewer. Refreshed annually.

## Equity condition

Both measures are reported with their ethnic composition and the remand-stage Relative Rate Index. A 25% reduction that leaves the Black and Mixed Heritage shares, or the RRI, unchanged is recorded as a volume success and a fairness failure. PRISM-R reports the two side by side.

## Not treated as progress

The share of the custody population on remand (44% in 2024-25), which is a ratio and can fall by sentencing more children. A fall in the stock that is not matched by the flow, which indicates shorter remands rather than fewer.

## Target date

Assessed at both the last full year of the Parliament (year ending March 2029) and the latest published month before dissolution.

Decided 3 September 2026. Reviewer sign-off pending.
